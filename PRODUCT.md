# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Invited family and friends reading on phones and desktop, in English or Hindi.

## Product Purpose
A private wedding invitation with server-authorized event visibility and, in a later phase, persistent guest responses.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The protected page consumes persisted bilingual content after guest authorization. No public group selector. All celebration dates use Asia/Kolkata. The invitation is operable by keyboard and with reduced motion. No audio. Personal content, real access links, reference artwork, and credentials remain outside public source.

## Brand Commitments
The approved invitation direction is refined watercolor stationery with generous whitespace, coral and sage, serif lettering, warm paper and restrained botanical edges.

## Evidence on Hand
The private approved mockup governs the direction. Official logo and approved photography are missing; show explicit neutral placeholders. Do not invent a monogram, people, architecture, venue imagery, ritual explanations, or dress codes. Original decorative botanicals are allowed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
English and Hindi throughout, readable Devanagari, 320px minimum viewport, keyboard opening/skip/replay, immediate reduced-motion access, text remains usable when artwork fails.
