# Invitation design

The approved world is warm watercolor stationery: an uncoated ivory sheet, coral serif names, delicate botanical corners and fine sage rules. It extends the existing global palette. This is an Experience surface that becomes a Read surface after opening.

The cover is a folded paper envelope with an unmarked seal and an explicit artwork placeholder. Opening lifts the flap over 1.2 seconds; skip is always immediate. The invitation follows in a continuous document: generous centered welcome, two family paragraphs, an editorial story and five-photo collage, grouped date lists, then venue and contact details. All event rows remain in the document, with date anchors for navigation.

Cormorant Garamond supplies display and italic lettering. Noto Sans Devanagari supplies body and Hindi text. No invented cursive Hindi. Controls use compact, legible text and drawn arrow linework. No dashboard cards, gradient panels, grand architectural illustrations or claim-bearing decorative imagery.

Original generated watercolor botanicals sit at the edges and never carry meaning. Neutral photo mats and the small dashed official-artwork placeholder clearly disclose missing inputs. Personal content only comes from authorized DTOs.

Locale state lives above a stable NextIntlClientProvider, with no locale key, so future form children keep their state. Navigation uses native anchors. Focus follows opening to the welcome heading and replay to the envelope; reduced motion bypasses the opening.
